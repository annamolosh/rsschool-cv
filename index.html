About me

I am currently studying frontend development through the Rolling Scopes School program. My goal is to gain practical skills and start a career in IT as a junior frontend developer. At this stage, I am building a solid foundation in web technologies and plan to continue with RS School Stage 1. I appreciate clear structure, enjoy learning, and try to combine technical thinking with creativity thanks to my background in engineering and design.
